# Round 18 — Windows checklist

The headless suite proves the logic on every push. These are the things only a real rig shows:
one line per item, what to do, and what you should see. Tick them on a Windows machine with the
full build (libVLC bundled), two displays and an audio interface. The round came from four
questions — a Fractals page like the Particles page, whether the particles need the fractals'
treatments, a faster start and restart, and what .NET 10 buys — so the rows are the studio, the
sim under load, the stopwatch at the start, and the runtime.

| 3 | A faster start and restart | Cold: start the portable exe from a stopped machine and read ADMIN → Machine's Start-up line; warm: close and start again; with outputs on and an NDI send running press RESTART APP (SHOW COMES BACK) and time it with a phone from the click to the outputs back on; click BUILD → Lower thirds within a second of the desk appearing; leave the desk for ten seconds and click every page on the rail; read patterns.watchdog.log after the restart | The Start-up line reads every phase — *runtime · settings · graphics · avalonia · services · view model · pages · window · first frame* — and the desk appears before the pages behind it are built (the window's *pages* phase is a fraction of what it was); the restart is seconds, not ten: the watchdog logs the exit the moment it happens (no second lost to a poll), the app comes up, and the show is back on as soon as the window has opened rather than 2.5 s later; the early click lands on a built page (a fault there would be logged, never a crash); ten seconds in, every page opens instantly; the exe is larger than 88 MB and starts faster than it did (the bundle is no longer decompressed on every start) |
