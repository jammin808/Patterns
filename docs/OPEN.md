# The still-open ledger

What a round found and did not fix, cumulative, from round 79 on. The rule: **nothing leaves the open
table until a row names the unit that closed it**; a closed row moves to the second table with that
unit, and a row is never deleted. A round's papers commit adds the rows it found ("found on the way",
the field's files, a code-lens pass) and moves the rows it closed. A read-only code-lens pass runs
between field reports and adds its rows tagged *never found* — the retrospective that seeded this
ledger found nine such items no round had seen.

The seed is the retrospective handed in at round 79 (its "What is still open at HEAD" table, each row
verified at `08dcaaa`) and `docs/PLAN.md` §96.8. Weights are the retrospective's after its skeptics'
corrections: *medium* is a fault a rig can meet, *low* a hygiene or a latent one.
