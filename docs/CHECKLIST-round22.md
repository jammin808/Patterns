# Round 22 — Windows checklist

The headless suite proves the record's shape, every path that moves it, and that a sink asks for
the frame that finishes a crossfade. These are the things only a real rig shows: one line per item,
what to do, and what you should see. Tick them on a Windows machine with the full build, two
displays, an NDI receiver on the network, and a show with a cue stack and a brand kit.

| # | Item | Do | Expect |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1 | The crash comes back to the show, not the edit | Outputs live on a walk-in look with EDIT SAFE **off**. Arm EDIT SAFE, build the next look in the preview (do not TAKE), then end the process from Task Manager | Within seconds the desk is back with the **walk-in look on the wall** and the half-built look still in the preview; EDIT SAFE is armed as you left it; the status line says the show was put back on |
| 2 | RESTART loses no more than a crash does | Same state as 1, plus a cue stack mid-run (fire 03.020). Machine page → RESTART | Identical to 1 — the wall keeps the walk-in look — *and* the Run surface comes back on 03.020, disarmed, with its history; press ARM to continue. Before this round, RESTART put the untaken preview on the wall and lost the caller's place |
| 3 | The brand kit is the client's, not tomorrow's | Client A's brand kit on air. EDIT SAFE on; load client B's kit on the Branding page (the wall correctly keeps A). Kill the process | The wall comes back in **client A's colours, font and logo**, the preview in client B's. A look carries no brand kit, so this is the item that proves the record is the program itself |
| 4 | The name on the wall is the right one | Lower third 'Speaker' on air with the person at the lectern. While it is up, prep the next name into the same design in the designer. Kill the process | The lower third comes back carrying **the name that was on air**, in the same place in its build-on, not the prepped one and not from zero |
| 5 | A darkened wall stays dark | Three screens live; FADE the foyer wall on its own. Kill the process | The foyer wall comes back **black**, the other two lit, and the desk's tally still shows it black. It never flashes the picture on the way |
| 6 | A locked screen keeps what it was reading | A confidence monitor (Role = Confidence, follows cues off) showing a countdown. EDIT SAFE on; change that screen's picture in the sandbox without sending. Kill the process | The stage monitor comes back on **the countdown**, not the un-taken change |
| 7 | The desk was editing live, and comes back editing live | Show set to start in EDIT SAFE. Turn EDIT SAFE **off** mid-show and edit live. Kill the process | The desk comes back with EDIT SAFE **off**: the next slider you touch reaches the audience, as it did before. Coming back split would swallow it silently |
| 8 | The stream is named, never restarted behind you | Stream live to a test endpoint. Kill the process | Outputs and the audio track come back; the stream does **not** start by itself, and the status line says the stream was live and to press STREAM |
| 9 | The desk knows what it calls the picture | Recall a look to air, load another into the preview with → PVW, fire a cue. Kill the process | The LIVE strip names the cue again, the Looks list lights **PROGRAM** on the look on air and **PREVIEW** on the loaded one, and LOOK BACK still goes back to the one before |
| 10 | The screens change hands without changing picture | With a desk live and EDIT SAFE open, launch Patterns again from the same folder (do not close the first) | The new desk takes the screens and the room's picture **does not change** — the walk-in look stays up, not the first desk's untaken preview. The status line names the takeover |
| 11 | A restart that does not come up cleanly still has its record | As 1, but make the relaunch fail once (rename a media file the show opens, then put it back) | The next start still finds the record and still puts the show back. The record is not deleted until the recovery that needs it has run |
| 12 | A Pattern Type change reaches the wall and the tiles | BUILD → Pattern with a still picture (Grid). Pick **Focus**, then **Colour bars**, then **Flat field**, touching nothing else between | Every switcher miniature, the PROGRAM and PREVIEW panes and every output **change on the press**, crossfading over the show's transition time and landing on the new picture. Before this round they sat on the old picture until some unrelated edit — on the desk *and*, for a look recall between two still pictures, on the wall |
| 13 | And the same with transitions off | Machine/Show: transitions off. Repeat 12 | The change is instant everywhere, with no crossfade — and still every sink |
| 14 | Nothing costs a frame | With two displays live, an NDI send, the stream and four wall tiles with MON on, sit on a still pattern for ten minutes, then work the Pattern page hard for two | Machine → STABILITY shows no recurring frames past 25 ms and no rise in the worst frame; the record on disk is rewritten only when the picture on air moves, not on every slider |
